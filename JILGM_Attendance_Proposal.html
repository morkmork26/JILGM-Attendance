Key Features

Everything your ministry needs for attendance management in one app

📷

Smart Check-In System

Multiple ways to check in members quickly and accurately on Sunday service.

  • QR Scan mode with front and back camera support
  • Quick Tap mode with visual avatar grid
  • Search mode with instant name matching
  • Visitor walk-in check-in (no pre-registration needed)
  • Audio beep confirmation on successful check-in
  • Duplicate prevention (cannot check in same member twice)
See details
👥

Member Management

Complete member database with 20+ fields, photos, and organized views.

  • Full profile: name, contact, address, birthday, gender, civil status
  • Ministry assignment, D-Group leader, classes attended
  • Emergency contact information
  • Photo capture and display
  • Filter by status (Active/Inactive), ministry, and outreach
  • Inline QR code display and resend capability
See details
⚡

Automatic Registration

Members register once via Google Form. Everything else is automatic.

  • Google Form captures all member information
  • Auto-generates unique Member ID (MEM-001, MEM-002...)
  • QR code auto-created and emailed to member
  • QR saved to Google Drive for backup
  • No manual data entry needed by ushers
See details
🔃

Real-Time Sync

All usher devices stay synchronized. Works offline and syncs when connected.

  • Auto-sync every 60 seconds (silent, no user action needed)
  • Offline check-ins queued and synced when internet returns
  • Installable as native app (PWA) on any phone
  • Multiple ushers can check in simultaneously
  • New form registrations appear automatically on all devices
See details
📊

Attendance Reports

Instant, detailed reports with demographic breakdowns and trends.

  • Age bracket breakdown: Youth, YPro, Mothers, Fathers, Children
  • Separate line items per outreach location
  • Weekly attendance bar chart (last 8 Sundays)
  • Ministry breakdown with progress bars
  • Follow-up list for members absent 3+ Sundays
  • One-tap email report to leadership
See details
🧠

Smart Attendance Logic

Automated intelligence to track member engagement and growth.

  • Auto-detects inactive members (8 consecutive Sundays absent)
  • Custom inactivity rules per outreach location
  • New member "N" badge for First Time and Returning Guests
  • Auto-graduation to Regular Attendee after 4 check-ins
  • Auto-reactivation when inactive member returns
  • Membership status pipeline: First Time > Returning > Regular
See details
🌏

Outreach Tracking

Track satellite locations separately with location-specific rules.

  • Four locations: Sto. Tomas, Tanauan, Naujan, Socorro
  • Tanauan: Tracked on 1st Sundays only (8-month inactivity rule)
  • Naujan/Socorro: No auto-inactive (grace for remote areas)
  • Separate reporting per outreach in attendance reports
  • Grand total separates main church vs outreach numbers
See details
🔒

Data Security & Backup

Your data stays in your own Google account with automated backups.

  • Google Sheets as database (accessible, familiar, exportable)
  • Weekly automated backup with email notification
  • QR codes stored in Google Drive
  • No third-party servers or paid subscriptions
  • Data owned 100% by the church
See details
